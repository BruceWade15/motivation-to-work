# Motivation to Work

A short assessment for staff and candidates. It looks at what motivates people to come to work and put effort into the vision of the company.

There are 10 questions, and it takes about 2 minutes. Each answer is scored from 1 to 5, for a total between 10 and 50. The result shows one of three bands: Low, Medium or High.

The page runs in the browser only. Names and answers are not saved or sent anywhere.
